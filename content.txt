ONCE THERE WAS A MAN.

HE WAS A VERY GOOD MAN, OR AT LEAST HE THOUGHT HE WAS. IN FACT, HE NEVER THOUGHT THAT HE COULD EVEN BE A BAD MAN—THE IDEA SIMPLY NEVER CROSSED HIS MIND. HOWEVER, HE WAS WRONG. AND HE WOULD FIND OUT VERY SOON.

ONE DAY, THIS MAN WHO WE WILL CALL “ZERO” WOKE UP TO THE SOUND OF A SCREAM. STARTLED (AS ONE RIGHTFULLY SHOULD BE), HE SPRUNG OUT OF BED AND DOWN THE STAIRS TO THE SOURCE OF THE SOUND. THERE, ON THE GROUND, WAS A LETTER. WHEN ZERO CAUTIOUSLY REACHED OUT TO READ THE NOTE, A WITHERED AND EMACIATED ARM EMERGED FROM THE DARK & GRASPED HIS WRIST. HE STARED INTO THE SHADOWS FROM WHICH IT EMERGED AND SAW THE OUTLINE OF A FACE, FEATURES ANCIENT AND SUNKEN.

“DON’T FORGET ME, FATHER,” SHE WHISPERED HOARSELY, “DON’T FORGET ME AS I FORGOT MYSELF.”
